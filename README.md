# QAF Inventory – marketing videos

Public hosting for our TikTok and Instagram videos (English and Kiswahili).

**How it works:** Claude makes each video, uploads the final MP4 here, and schedules it on Metricool using the GitHub Pages link. Old videos are deleted after they're posted, to stay under the 1 GB Pages limit.

**Everything here is public on purpose.** These videos are going public on social media anyway. Never put app code, keys, customer data or anything private in this repo.

Accounts: TikTok & Instagram @qafinventory.eng and @qafinventory.sw
